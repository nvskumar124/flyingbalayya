# 🦁 FLYING BALAYYA - BALAYYA BABU GAME

A complete, self-contained, high-performance HTML5 canvas game inspired by the viral **Flying Balayya** meme game starring **Nandamuri Balakrishna (God of Masses)**.

---

## 🚀 How to Play:
1. Double-click **`index.html`** (or **`Flying_Balayya_Game.html`**).
2. It opens directly in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave, mobile browsers).
3. **Zero server setup required** — 100% offline and standalone with all audio and visuals embedded.

---

## 🎮 Controls:
* **Tap Screen / Click Mouse / Spacebar**: Flap and fly Balayya's medallion avatar.
* **Dodge Obstacles**: Avoid the top (inverted) and bottom Mansion House French Brandy bottles!
* **Mute Toggle (🔊 / 🔇)**: Click top-left button to mute or unmute.
* **Skin Toggle (🕶️ / 🦁)**: Click top-right button to switch between Classic Balayya and Swag Shades Balayya.

---

## 🎵 The 3 Audio Tracks Included:
1. **Audio 3 (After Entering the Game)**:
   * *"Don't trouble the trouble! If you trouble the trouble, trouble troubles you! I am not the trouble, I am the TRUTH!"*
   * Plays when you tap **"ENTER THE GAME"**.
2. **Audio 2 (While Playing)**:
   * *"Nee kanti choopulloki na pranam cherinde emayam chesave..."*
   * Balayya's singing track loops seamlessly during gameplay.
3. **Audio 1 (When Death)**:
   * *"Sorry, ila matladakoodadu... LANJA KODAKA! Guddaloodadeesi kodatha!"*
   * Plays with screen shake and red flash upon collision.
